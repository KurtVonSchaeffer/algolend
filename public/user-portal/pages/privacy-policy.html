Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  |  AlgoLend (Pty) Ltd

1. Who We Are

AlgoLend (Pty) Ltd ("AlgoLend", "we", "us") is a registered credit provider (NCR: NCRCP13510) and authorised financial services provider (FSP 53423). We are the responsible party for your personal information as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

Information Officer: info@algolend.co.za

2. What Information We Collect

  • Identity information: Full name, ID number, date of birth, gender
  • Contact details: Mobile number, email address, physical and postal address
  • Financial information: Income, expenses, existing debt, bank account details, bank transaction history (via open banking)
  • Business information: Company name, registration number, director details, CIPC data
  • Credit information: Credit bureau reports, payment history, defaults
  • Biometric information: Liveness selfie and facial match (processed by Experian and verified against the Department of Home Affairs)
  • Documents: Uploaded company documents, bank statements, financial statements
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, browser, session timestamps

3. Why We Collect Your Information

  • To process and assess your credit application (National Credit Act obligation)
  • To verify your identity and conduct AML/PEP screening (FICA obligation)
  • To verify your Home Affairs identity record (FICA/CDD obligation)
  • To assess your affordability (NCA s.81 obligation)
  • To manage your loan account, process payments, and send statements
  • To report to the South African Credit and Risk Reporting Association (SACRRA) as required by the NCA
  • To send you account notifications, payment reminders, and service updates
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Who We Share Your Information With

  • Experian South Africa: Credit bureau verification, identity verification (KYC), biometric liveness verification, and AML/PEP screening
  • TruID: Open banking — read-only access to your bank transaction data for affordability assessment
  • SureSystems: DebiCheck mandate registration for payment collection
  • SACRRA / Credit bureaus: Mandatory credit reporting as required by the NCA
  • Supabase: Secure cloud database hosting (data stored in South Africa)
  • Regulatory authorities and law enforcement where required by law

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Active loan accounts: Duration of the loan plus 5 years after settlement
  • Declined applications: 3 years from the date of decision
  • AML / FICA records: 5 years from the date of the last transaction (FICA s.23)
  • Biometric data: Retained only for the duration required by Experian IDMN for audit trail purposes, then deleted

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Object to the processing of your information in certain circumstances
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this will not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforeg@justice.gov.za

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer at info@algolend.co.za. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is restricted to authorised personnel only.

8. Cookies

We use session cookies solely to maintain your authenticated session. We do not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification. Continued use of the platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
